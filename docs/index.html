Why this exists

The fake sponsorship email is now the #1 way creator channels get stolen.

A “brand” emails you. The rate is great. The brief is a password-protected ZIP, or the contract needs you to “sign in with Google” to view it. One click later an infostealer has your session cookies and your channel is streaming crypto scams. It hits creators with 5k subscribers and 5M alike — and Gmail’s spam filter waves most of it through because the domain is brand new.

The “brief” is malware

Archives and installers dressed up as campaign briefs, game builds or press kits, sent from lookalike domains like nordvpn-partners.co.

The “contract” is a login page

“Sign in to view the agreement” takes your Google password and 2FA in one go. Channel gone in minutes.

The real ones bury the terms

Perpetual usage rights, all-media whitelisting and Net 90 hide in paragraph six. Creators sign away far more than a post.

FAQ

Questions creators ask us

Does LegitPitch read my email?

Only on your screen. It runs as a browser extension on the Gmail tab you already have open and analyses the message you are looking at, locally. There is no server, no Gmail API access and no OAuth “read all your mail” permission to grant. Nothing is uploaded.

How is this different from Gmail’s own spam filter?

Gmail catches known bad senders. Sponsorship scams use freshly registered lookalike domains and clean-looking archives, so most land in the inbox. LegitPitch understands the context — that an email claiming to be NordVPN isn’t from a NordVPN domain, or that a “brief” shouldn’t be a password-protected ZIP.

Will it work with a Google Workspace / custom-domain address?

Yes. Workspace uses the same Gmail interface, so LegitPitch works on you@yourbrand.com exactly as on @gmail.com.

What about Instagram or TikTok DMs?

Not at launch. Paste any DM into the free Pitch Checker and you get the same analysis. In-app DM support is on the roadmap.

Can a real brand be flagged by mistake?

Yes — that’s why the badge says Unverified rather than Scam unless the signals are unambiguous (lookalike domain plus a malware-style attachment, for example). You can mark a sender as trusted; that choice stays on your device.

Which browsers?

Chrome and Edge at launch (any Chromium browser that can install from the Chrome Web Store). Firefox is planned.
