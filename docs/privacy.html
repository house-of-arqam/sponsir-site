Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026 · Bowncr is made by House of Arqam Ventures LLC.

The short version

  • The Pitch Checker on this site runs entirely in your browser. The text you paste is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The waitlist stores your email address and the page you signed up from. That is all.
  • We keep one anonymous counter of how many checks were run and their risk level (low / medium / high / critical). No content, no IP address, no cookies.
  • This site sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or analytics.

Pitch Checker

The analysis engine is a JavaScript file served from this site. When you click “Check this pitch”, it processes the text inside your browser tab and shows the result. There is no network request containing the email. You can verify this in your browser’s developer tools (Network tab) or by reading the source.

Waitlist

If you join the waitlist we store your email address, the page you signed up from (for example “home” or “checker”) and the time of signup, in a Cloudflare Workers KV datastore in the account of House of Arqam Ventures LLC. We use it to email you about Bowncr’s launch and your founding-member offer. We do not sell, rent or share it. Every email we send includes a one-click unsubscribe; you can also write to hello@bowncr.app and we will delete your address.

To protect the form from abuse we rate-limit requests by network address for a short period. The address is not stored with your signup.

Anonymous usage counter

When a check completes, the page sends a request containing only the word “check” and the resulting risk level. We use these counts to understand whether the tool is useful. Nothing in that request identifies you or the email you checked.

The browser extension

The Bowncr extension is not yet released. When it is, this policy will be updated before launch. Its design commitment is the same as the checker: analysis happens on the Gmail page in your browser, it does not use the Gmail API or Google OAuth, and it does not upload the contents of your email. Licensing (for the paid tier) exchanges only a license key and an anonymous device identifier with our licensing server.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and the waitlist endpoint on Cloudflare Workers. Those providers process requests to deliver the service and keep standard server logs under their own privacy policies.
