Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Sponsir ("the extension") is made by House of Arqam Ventures LLC ("we"). This policy describes what the extension and this website do with data. The short version: the extension and the free Pitch Checker work locally in your browser, we do not run analytics or tracking, and the only data that leaves your browser is what is needed to sell and verify a license, your email address if you join the waitlist, and an anonymous verdict-level count when you use the Pitch Checker.

Data the extension processes

The extension runs only on mail.google.com. When you open a message, it reads what Gmail has already rendered on the page — the sender name and address, Reply-To, subject, visible body text, links, attachment names and Gmail's "signed-by" / "mailed-by" indicators — and analyses it inside your browser tab to show a verdict and extract deal terms. It does not use the Gmail API or Google sign-in, does not read messages you have not opened, and never sends message content to us or to any third party.

Data stored on your device

  • Your settings and the extension's working data, in the browser's extension storage.
  • Deals you choose to track (brand, sender, subject, rate, deliverables, deadline, stage, reminder date, notes and a link to the Gmail thread), stored locally in the extension. Nothing about a deal is uploaded.
  • A count of how many messages had their terms extracted this month, on the free plan.
  • A random installation identifier, used to count your license's devices.
  • Your license key and a short-lived entitlement token, if you have a trial or Pro license. The license key is also saved to your browser's sync storage so it follows you to your other signed-in browsers.

Uninstalling the extension removes all of this.

Data sent to our licensing service

When you start a trial, buy Pro, or activate a license, the extension contacts our licensing service (a Cloudflare Worker at license.sponsir.app). It sends only what is needed for that action: the random installation identifier, an anonymous visitor identifier (for trials), the plan you chose, and your license key. The service stores your license status and the list of installation identifiers using it. It never receives page content or browsing activity.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Paddle, our merchant of record. Paddle collects your payment details and billing address, handles taxes and invoices, and tells us when a purchase completes so we can issue your license key. We never see your card number. Paddle's handling of your data is described in Paddle's privacy policy.

Email

After a purchase we email your license key to the address you gave Paddle, via Resend. We keep your email address alongside your license record so we can help you recover a key. We do not send marketing email to buyers.

This website

This site is static and sets no cookies. It uses no analytics or advertising trackers.

  • Pitch Checker. Text you paste into the free checker is analysed in your browser and is never uploaded. After a check, the page sends a single anonymous event to our Worker (the verdict level — low, medium, high or critical — and nothing else) so we can count how often the checker is used.
  • Waitlist. If you join the waitlist we store your email address and the page you signed up from, and send one confirmation email via Resend. We email waitlist members only about the launch; reply to any message to be removed.
  • Checkout, license key and device pages. These call our licensing service as described above. The checkout page loads Paddle's checkout script; no other third-party code runs on this site.

Data retention and deletion

License records are kept while your license is active and for a reasonable period afterwards for refund and fraud handling. Waitlist entries are deleted after launch. Email hello@sponsir.app to have your license record, waitlist entry or email address deleted.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes and adjust the date above.
